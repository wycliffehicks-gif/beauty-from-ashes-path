APP PURPOSE

The app is for adults who know something feels emotionally or spiritually heavy, buried, disconnected, or stuck, but do not know how to begin working with it.

The app should help users move gradually from:

- Confusion toward awareness
- Hidden pain toward honest naming
- Shame toward dignity and compassion
- Avoidance toward gentle engagement
- Survival patterns toward greater choice
- Isolation toward safe connection
- Fragmentation toward integration
- Hopelessness toward renewed possibility
- Feeling stuck toward one honest next step

It must not promise to cure trauma, depression, grief, addiction, mental illness, or spiritual struggle.

It is an educational, reflective, spiritually sensitive, and practical healing resource—not psychotherapy, medical treatment, crisis support, or emergency care.

RELATIONSHIP TO THE BEAUTY FROM ASHES ECOSYSTEM

The app should complement, but not merely duplicate:

1. The Beauty from Ashes YouTube videos
2. The planned guided journal:
   “Beauty from Ashes: 30 Days of Healing Reflection”
3. A future online guided course
4. Reflection cards and other Resurgence Therapeutics healing tools

Each format should have a distinct purpose:

- Videos provide teaching and emotional connection
- The journal provides deeper written reflection
- The app supports daily participation, practice, reminders, and progress
- The course provides structured explanation and guided learning

POSSIBLE APP FEATURES TO EVALUATE

Do not assume all of these belong in the first version. Recommend the smallest useful version first.

Consider:

- Gentle daily emotional check-in
- “What am I carrying today?” reflection
- Daily Beauty from Ashes teaching
- One Honest Step practice
- Guided journalling with saved private entries
- Audio reflections, prayers, grounding practices, or blessings
- Optional Scripture and spiritually explicit reflections
- Non-coercive alternative reflections for spiritually open users
- Mood, energy, connection, and hope tracking
- Personal patterns and insights
- Favourite or saved reflections
- Progress through a 30-day pathway
- Gentle reminders chosen by the user
- A personal support and safety plan
- A “pause and ground” tool
- A “name what is happening” tool
- A “this is now, not then” trauma-sensitive practice
- Grief and lament exercises
- Shame and self-worth reflections
- Safe relationship and support prompts
- End-of-week review
- Closing integration and continuation plan
- Links to Resurgence Therapeutics resources and professional support
- Clear crisis and emergency guidance based on the user’s location, without presenting the app as crisis care

VOICE AND EXPERIENCE

The app should feel like:

Psychotherapy meets spiritual direction meets thoughtful public teaching.

The experience should be:

- Warm
- Human
- Calm
- Compassionate
- Emotionally credible
- Psychologically informed
- Spiritually grounded
- Hopeful without forced positivity
- Simple to navigate
- Accessible to people who are emotionally tired
- Dignified rather than clinical or childish

Avoid:

- Gamifying suffering
- Shame-based streaks
- Punishing users for missed days
- Overloading users with too many questions
- Robotic AI language
- Generic wellness clichés
- Bright, noisy, overstimulating screens
- Preaching
- Spiritual bypassing
- Promising transformation
- Diagnosing users
- Presenting automated responses as psychotherapy
- Encouraging deep trauma processing without safety, pacing, or professional support

CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The app should not treat people as problems to be fixed.

It should help users notice, name, understand, and respond compassionately to what they are carrying.

The central daily movement could be:

NOTICE
What is happening within me?

NAME
What may I be carrying?

UNDERSTAND
What might this feeling or pattern be protecting?

RECONNECT
What do I need from myself, another person, life, or God?

TAKE ONE HONEST STEP
What small, realistic action can I take today?

Evaluate whether this should become the central app structure.

POSSIBLE BUSINESS MODEL

Evaluate options such as:

- One modest one-time app purchase
- App access included with the physical or digital journal
- Journal + app bundle
- App + guided course bundle
- Low-cost annual access
- Institutional or church/community licences later

Do not choose a subscription merely because apps often use subscriptions. Recommend what best fits the audience, product, and early-stage business.

Use Canadian English.

Begin only with the product strategy, user journey, minimum viable product, and screen map.
